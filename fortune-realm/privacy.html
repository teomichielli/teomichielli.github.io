Privacy Policy

Fortune Realm: Coin Arcade for iPhone and iPad. Last updated 13 October 2026.

This policy explains what information the game handles. The game is made by Teo Michielli, an independent developer. Contact: teomichielli08@gmail.com.

What the game itself stores

There is no account, sign-up or password. Your coins, levels, progress and settings are stored only on your device and are removed when you delete the app. The game does not sell personal information and has no in-app purchases.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The game shows optional rewarded video ads and occasional ad breaks through Google AdMob. When an ad is requested, Google may collect and use:

  • device identifiers, including the advertising identifier (IDFA) only if you allow tracking when asked
  • your IP address, which indicates your approximate location
  • which ads were shown and whether you interacted with them
  • diagnostic data such as device model, system version and crash information

Google uses this to deliver and measure ads and to prevent fraud. Where required (for example in the EEA, UK and Switzerland) the game asks for your consent through Google's consent message, and you can change it later from Settings > Ad Privacy Choices. You can also turn off personalised ads with Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking on your device.

Analytics (Google Analytics for Firebase)

The game uses Firebase Analytics to understand how it is played, for example which games are opened, level-ups, bonus rounds and ad views. Firebase may collect an app-instance identifier for this installation, gameplay events and device information such as model, system version, language and country. This is used in aggregate to improve the game, not to identify you.

Invite codes (Firebase Authentication and Cloud Firestore)

To make invite codes work, the game creates an anonymous Firebase user ID for your installation (no name, email or phone number). It stores your 6-character code with that ID, and when you redeem a friend's code it stores which code you used, so each player can redeem once and the inviter can claim their reward.

Game Center

If you are signed in to Apple Game Center, your weekly coins won and your highest level are sent to the Fortune Realm leaderboards. This is handled by Apple under your Game Center settings and Apple's privacy policy.

Notifications

If you allow notifications, the game schedules local reminders on your device (for example when free coins are ready). They are created on your device; no notification data is sent to us.

Age

Fortune Realm is intended only for adults aged 18 and over and is not directed to children. We do not knowingly collect information from anyone under 18.

Retention and deletion

Game data on your device is deleted when you delete the app. To ask for deletion of your invite-code record, email us with your invite code and we will remove it. Google's data handling is described in the Google Privacy Policy and how Google uses information from apps that use its services.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new date.
