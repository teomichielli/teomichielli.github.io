Wildstead

Support

Wildstead is a free pixel-art adventure farm game for iPhone. Explore the wild, tame animals, bring them home and grow your farm, town and zoo.

Common questions

Is my progress saved?
Yes, automatically, on your device. There is no account. Deleting the app deletes your progress, and it does not transfer between devices.

How do I tame an animal?
Walk up to it in the wild and tap Tame. Tap when the moving ring lines up with the green band. Rarer animals need more hits and move faster. Better bait from the Bait Shop makes the band wider.

An animal got away. Can I try again?
You can watch an optional ad for another try. Once a day the Shop also gives a Wonder Fruit, which catches any animal for certain.

How do I earn more coins?
Rarer, heavier and higher-level animals earn more. Upgrade pens, train animals, fuse three of a kind, keep them happy and put spare animals on show in the Zoo.

I keep getting knocked out in the wild.
Buy a better weapon and armour at the Blacksmith before exploring a new biome, carry potions and bring a battle companion from the Barn.

How do I get around faster?
Ride one of your animals. Tap the saddle button in the wild.

Why do I see ads?
Ads are optional. They only play when you tap a button that offers a reward for watching one.

Does the game cost money?
No. There are no purchases in the game.
