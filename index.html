Support and Privacy

Support

Questions, bugs or ideas? Email teomichielli08@gmail.com and you'll get a reply within a few days. For bug reports, include your iPhone model, the city number and a screenshot.

How do I play?Connect every house with one unbroken road. Tap a house to park your car, then drag to the house next door. Your road can never cross itself, except on bridges.
I'm stuck.Tap Hint to fill in the next move for 15 coins, or Watch to see the solution after a short ad. You can also drag backwards to undo.
Does it cost money?No. The game is free with nothing to buy. Coins are earned by playing, and ads are optional.
Will I lose my progress?Progress is saved on your device only. Deleting the app erases it, and it does not transfer between devices.
Sound, music or ad choices?Sound and music are in Settings. For ads, go to iPhone Settings, Privacy and Security, Tracking.

Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 17, 2026

One Line City (the game) is made by Teo Michielli. This policy explains what happens to information when you play.

What stays on your device

Your progress, coins, cars, roads, trails and settings are saved only on your device. There are no accounts and no game servers, so we never receive or store this information.

Advertising

The game shows optional rewarded video ads through Google AdMob. To show and measure ads, Google may process your device advertising identifier, IP address, approximate location, device information and ad interaction data.

On iPhone you are asked whether the game may track you. If you decline, ads still appear but are not personalized. You can change this any time in Settings, Privacy and Security, Tracking. Where required by law, a consent screen appears before personalized ads are shown. See how Google uses this data.

Children

The game is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them.

Your choices

The game keeps no account, so there is nothing for us to delete on request. Deleting the app removes everything stored on your device.

Changes

Updates to this policy will be posted here with a new date.
