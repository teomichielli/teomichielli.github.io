Support

Questions, bugs or ideas? Email teomichielli08@gmail.com and you'll get a reply within a few days. For bug reports, include your device model, what you were doing and a screenshot if you can.

How do battles work?Build a squad of 3 heroes in SQUAD. Battles are turn-based: faster heroes act more often. Each hero has a basic attack, a class skill and an ultimate that charges as they fight. AUTO plays for you; switch it off to pick cards yourself.
How do I get more heroes?Use SUMMON, open chests from the Shop, the free chest on the home screen and the daily login calendar. Duplicate heroes become shards used to level up and add stars.
How do clubs work?Reach Gold rank to create a club. Anyone can join public clubs from the directory; invite-only clubs need an invite code from a member. Clubs hold up to 30 members and unlock perks as they grow.
My progress is gone.Progress is saved on your device. Clearing the app's data or reinstalling can reset it. Email us with your coach name and we'll help where we can.
A purchase didn't arrive.Restart the game first. If it still hasn't arrived, contact us with the purchase receipt. Refunds are handled by the Apple App Store or Google Play under their policies.

Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 29, 2026

This policy explains what information Pocket Arena ("the game", "we") collects and how it is used.

Information stored on your device

Your game progress (heroes, currencies, settings and stats) is stored locally on your device.

Online features

If you play online, the game stores your coach name, trophies, defense squad, club membership and battle results on our game server so other players can battle your squad, see leaderboards and join clubs. Your coach name, trophies and club are visible to other players. We do not ask for your real name, email, phone number or location.

Advertising

The game shows ads through Google AdMob, including optional rewarded ads you choose to watch. AdMob may collect and use your device's advertising identifier, IP address and general usage data to show ads, measure performance and prevent fraud, and, where you have given consent, to personalize ads. Where required by law you will be asked for consent, and you can change your choice at any time. Learn more in Google's advertising policy and Google's Privacy Policy. You can reset or limit your advertising ID in your device settings.

Purchases

In-app purchases are processed by Apple or Google. We never receive or store your payment details.

How we use information

Only to run the game, provide online features, show ads, fix bugs and prevent cheating. We do not sell your personal information.

Children

The game is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has provided information, contact us and we will delete it.

Data retention and deletion

Online profile data is kept while your account is active. To have your online data deleted, email us with your coach name.

Changes

We may update this policy; the date above shows the latest version.
