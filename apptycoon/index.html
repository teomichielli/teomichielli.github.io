Support

Ship It! Idle App Tycoon is a free pixel-art idle game for iPhone. Type your own app ideas, ship them, hire a team, and grow from a bedroom into a Tower HQ.

Contact

Questions, bugs or feedback: teomichielli08@gmail.com. I usually reply within a few days. Please include your iPhone model and iOS version, and a screenshot if you can.

Common questions

Is my progress saved?
Yes, automatically, on your device. There is no account. Deleting the app deletes your progress, and it does not transfer between devices.

How are my app ideas scored?
Reviewers look at your genre, audience, size, how it makes money, and the words in your name and pitch. Trending genres and words score higher. Clear, specific pitches beat vague ones.

Why did my app stop growing?
Apps that score under 40 go obsolete when their launch wave ends. Ship an update that scores 40 or more to bring it back. Good apps keep growing forever, faster after ads and updates.

How do I get a bigger studio?
Earn downloads to level up, then buy the next room on the roadmap: Bedroom, Garage, Apartment, Warehouse, Office building, Tower HQ.

What does the helicopter do?
Tap it. Visitors drop in with cash, chests, download boosts or investor offers.

Why do I see ads?
Ads are optional. They only play when you tap a button that offers a reward, such as skipping a review or an extra daily spin.

Does the game cost money?
No. There are no purchases in the game.
